AI Provider Cache Clear Tool

Why do you need this?

We fixed a bug where configured providers (like OpenAI with masked API keys) were being filtered out of the frontend display. However, your browser may have cached the old filtered data.

Click the button below to clear the cache and allow the frontend to fetch the complete list of providers from the backend.

After clearing the cache:

  1. Go back to your AI Providers page
  2. Refresh the page (F5 or Ctrl+R)
  3. You should now see all 9 providers, including the configured OpenAI provider
